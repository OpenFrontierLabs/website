The open frontier

A more open foundation for frontier AI.

Community-driven infrastructure to train and advance world-class models in public, with every step logged and every result shared. None of it is built yet.

Open Compute

Pool GPUs from many contributors and donated clusters into one training fabric.

Open Training

DiLoCo-style pretraining and asynchronous RL with open tools, open data and open recipes.

Open Weights

Every checkpoint and the final weights are planned for release under Apache-2.0.

Heterogeneous Participation

Contribute what you have, from a single consumer GPU to an 8-GPU node.

How it works

From idle compute to open frontier models.

Islands of GPUs train locally for many steps, then sync compressed updates rarely. Slow links cost little, and nodes can join or leave without stopping the run. This is the design; none of it has run yet.

  1. Your Idle Compute

    A spare machine or GPU, offered to a run.

  3. Distributed Training Network

    Islands will join through a small coordinator and object storage.

  5. Shared Checkpoints

    Every checkpoint will be published as the run goes.

  7. Open Models

    Final weights will be open for anyone to use, build on and improve.

  9. Public Evidence

    Configs, logs and a credit ledger will be open to check.
